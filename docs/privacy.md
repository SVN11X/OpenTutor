# Privacy policy

OpenTutor Guardian keeps everything on your computer.

- It stores results, sessions, tasks and evidence in one SQLite file under `%LOCALAPPDATA%\OpenTutorGuardian`, plus a log file there.
- It sends nothing over the network. The only connection it can make is to Ollama on `127.0.0.1:11434`, and only if you choose Ollama.
- It has no telemetry, no accounts and no advertising.
- It does not record keys, the screen, audio, the camera or window titles.
- Files you hand in as evidence are read on your machine and are not uploaded.
- To remove your data, uninstall the program and delete the folder above.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/SVN11X/OpenTutor/issues.
